### 2.1 Construction of the Butyrate Biosynthetic Pathway

*Bacillus megaterium* But-0 was used as the starting strain for stepwise metabolic engineering. Using CRISPR-Cas9-mediated homologous recombination, genes associated with competing by-product pathways were progressively deleted, while heterologous genes involved in butyrate biosynthesis were integrated at the corresponding chromosomal loci. These modifications were designed to reduce by-product formation and enhance butyrate production (Fig. 1A).

To reduce lactate formation and reinforce the butyrate biosynthetic pathway, the lactate dehydrogenase gene *ldhA* was deleted from But-0, and the *Escherichia coli*-derived *atoB* gene encoding acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase was integrated at the same locus, yielding strain But-1. The butyrate titer increased from 10.53 to 11.63 g/L (10.44%), whereas lactate concentration decreased from 4.30 to 1.03 g/L (75.97%; Fig. 1B). The OD₆₀₀ decreased from 14.97 to 12.37 (17.37%), while the mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids increased from 41.7% to 47.7% (Fig. 1B,C). Acetate and succinate nevertheless remained at 5.23 and 6.47 g/L, respectively, indicating that substantial by-product accumulation persisted after the first modification.

The next modification targeted acetate formation. The acetate kinase gene *ackA* was deleted from But-1, and the *hbd* gene encoding 3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase from *Clostridium acetobutylicum* was integrated at the *ackA* locus to generate But-2 (Fig. 1A). Compared with its parent But-1, But-2 showed a higher butyrate titer of 12.67 g/L (8.88% increase), accompanied by a decrease in acetate concentration from 5.23 to 3.50 g/L (33.12%). Accordingly, the butyrate-to-acetate mass concentration ratio (B/A) increased from 2.22 to 3.62 (Fig. 1B). Lactate and succinate concentrations also declined, from 1.03 to 0.60 g/L and from 6.47 to 4.73 g/L, respectively. The mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids reached 58.9%, while OD₆₀₀ increased from 12.37 to 13.77 (11.32%; Fig. 1B,C). Thus, the combined deletion and integration were associated with increased butyrate accumulation, lower acetate accumulation, and a greater contribution of butyrate to the measured organic-acid pool.

To target the ethanol-forming branch, the aldehyde/alcohol dehydrogenase gene *adhE* was deleted from But-2, and the *C. acetobutylicum*-derived *crt* gene encoding 3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase was integrated at the same locus, producing But-3 (Fig. 1A). This modification resulted in only a marginal increase in butyrate titer, from 12.67 to 12.73 g/L (0.53%; Fig. 1B). Although acetate concentration decreased from 3.50 to 2.83 g/L (19.05%), lactate and succinate concentrations increased from 0.60 to 0.97 g/L and from 4.73 to 5.17 g/L, respectively. The mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids remained close to that in But-2 (58.7% versus 58.9%; Fig. 1C), whereas OD₆₀₀ increased from 13.77 to 15.17 (10.17%; Fig. 1B). Hence, this combined modification was accompanied by a higher OD₆₀₀ and lower acetate accumulation, but little additional improvement in butyrate titer or its share of the measured organic acids.

To target the pyruvate formate-lyase branch and strengthen the reduction step of the butyrate pathway, the *pflB* gene was deleted from But-3 and the *ter* gene encoding trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase from *C. acetobutylicum* was integrated at that locus, yielding But-4 (Fig. 1A). The butyrate titer increased from 12.73 to 13.53 g/L (6.28%), while lactate and acetate concentrations decreased to 0.77 and 2.50 g/L, respectively (Fig. 1B). However, succinate concentration increased from 5.17 to 6.77 g/L (30.97%), the highest level observed among strains But-0 through But-5. Correspondingly, the mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids declined from 58.7% to 57.4% (Fig. 1C), and OD₆₀₀ decreased from 15.17 to 14.13 (6.81%; Fig. 1B). Thus, the increase in butyrate production was accompanied by pronounced succinate accumulation, providing a rationale for targeting the succinate-forming branch in the subsequent construction step.

To address succinate accumulation and reinforce the terminal step of butyrate synthesis, the fumarate reductase gene cluster *frdABCD* was deleted from But-4, and the *E. coli*-derived *tesB* gene encoding acyl-CoA thioesterase II was integrated at the corresponding locus, generating But-5 (Fig. 1A). The butyrate titer increased from 13.53 to 17.23 g/L (27.34%), while succinate concentration decreased from 6.77 to 2.43 g/L (64.04%; Fig. 1B). Lactate and acetate concentrations further declined to 0.57 and 2.10 g/L, respectively, whereas OD₆₀₀ changed only slightly, from 14.13 to 14.43 (2.12%). Across the first five rounds of engineering, the total concentration of measured by-products decreased from 14.73 g/L in But-0 to 5.10 g/L in But-5 (65.38%; Fig. 1D). Meanwhile, the mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids increased from 57.4% in But-4 to 77.2% in But-5, which was 35.5 percentage points higher than that in But-0 (41.7%; Fig. 1C). These results showed that the combined *frdABCD* deletion and *tesB* integration were accompanied by a substantial reduction in succinate and total by-product accumulation, together with a higher butyrate titer and little change in OD₆₀₀.

To further strengthen butyrate synthesis while targeting branches associated with acetate formation, alanine metabolism, oxaloacetate replenishment, and methylglyoxal formation, five additional deletion-and-integration modifications were carried out sequentially in the But-5 background (Fig. 1A). The *poxB*, *pta*, *alaT*, *ppc*, and *mgsA* genes were deleted, and *atoB* from *E. coli*, *hbd* and *crt* from *C. acetobutylicum*, *ter* from *Treponema denticola*, and *tesB* from *E. coli* were integrated at the respective loci. The additional *ter* gene at the *ppc* locus, together with the previously integrated *ter* gene at the *pflB* locus, was intended to reinforce the reduction of crotonyl-CoA. The resulting strain, But-6, produced 22.07 g/L butyrate, representing increases of 28.05% over But-5 (17.23 g/L) and 109.49% over the starting strain But-0 (10.53 g/L; Fig. 1B). Its OD₆₀₀ decreased from 14.43 in But-5 to 13.10 (9.24%). Despite this decrease in OD₆₀₀, But-6 achieved the highest butyrate titer among the engineered strains and was therefore selected as the parent for subsequent sequential ARTP–UV–ARTP mutagenesis.

### C. 科学与语言风险复核

1. **组合编辑的归因**：But-1至But-5均是“敲除一个竞争支路基因 + 在该位点整合外源途径基因”；But-6包含五组**追加**编辑。本文只报告每轮总体效应，不声称各单基因的独立贡献。
2. **But-3与But-4不是常规增产叙述**：But-3有乳酸/琥珀酸回升和丁酸极小增幅；But-4丁酸提高同时琥珀酸明显积累。不能自动归因于代谢补偿或胞内碳流重分配。
3. **“显著”与统计学**：中文稿中“显著降低”“显著改善”等尚未从原始显著性检验输出核实，采用 *substantial/pronounced/increased/decreased* 等描述性词，不自行添加 *significantly*。
4. **OD₆₀₀的含义**：仅表示光密度读数；不声称是细胞比生长速率、活细胞计数或干重。
5. **选择性指标**：B/A为丁酸/乙酸的质量浓度比，丁酸质量分数为丁酸在测定有机酸总质量中的比例；不能相互替换，也不能把“质量分数提高35.5个百分点”误写成“提高35.5%”。
6. **图表**：Figure 1中A、B、C、D的功能按当前Word和已锁定英文图注匹配；未编辑图注，尚未从最终可编辑原图及底层数据验证坐标、误差、统计标识。
7. **术语**：暂用 *Bacillus megaterium*、*titer*、*chromosomal integration*、*mass fraction among measured organic acids*；基因斜体，菌名斜体，来源用文字解释。目标期刊与最终基因上标风格待作者决定。
8. **逻辑与文献学习**：参考[Saini 2014](https://doi.org/10.1021/jf500355p)、[Guo 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00253-021-11385-w)、[Li 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/bit.70018)的结果推动论证方式，**没有**借用他们的菌株、浓度、实验方法或机制测量作为本研究数据。相关论证解剖见[Undermind第三批原文阅读记录](https://app.undermind.ai/projects/41e498ee-4107-4ba8-b971-17a08590d3a7?path=/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87%E6%8A%95%E7%A8%BF%E8%AF%AD%E6%96%99/%E7%AC%AC%E4%B8%89%E6%89%B9%E5%AF%BC%E5%B8%88%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87%E8%8C%83%E6%96%87%E7%BB%93%E6%9E%9C%E8%AE%BA%E8%AF%81%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96_3%E7%AF%87.md)。
