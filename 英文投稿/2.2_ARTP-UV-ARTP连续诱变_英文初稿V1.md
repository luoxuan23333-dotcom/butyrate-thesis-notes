## I. English draft — Section 2.2 (revised in place)

### 2.2 Enhanced Butyrate Production through Sequential ARTP–UV–ARTP Mutagenesis

The first round of atmospheric and room-temperature plasma (ARTP) mutagenesis was performed using engineered strain But-6 as the parent (Fig. 2A). In primary tube screening, 100 candidate mutants were tested alongside a separate But-6 control, which produced 2.39 g/L butyrate under the same screening conditions. Of these candidates, 16 exceeded the parental control, corresponding to 16.00% of those screened (Fig. 2B,C). But-6A100, the highest-producing candidate in the tube screen, was selected for independent shake-flask validation. Its butyrate titer reached 27.73 g/L, a 25.68% increase over the parental But-6 (22.07 g/L). Final OD₆₀₀ also increased by 70.74%, from 13.10 to 22.37 (Fig. 2D). But-6A100 was therefore advanced as the parent for the subsequent UV mutagenesis round.

Using But-6A100 as the parent, the second round applied ultraviolet (UV) mutagenesis (Fig. 2A). The parental control produced 3.09 g/L butyrate in the tube-screening assay, and 26 of the 100 candidate mutants produced more than this control (26.00%; Fig. 2B,C). The top candidate, But-6B100, was subsequently assessed in independent shake-flask fermentation. Its butyrate titer increased by 15.14% relative to But-6A100, from 27.73 to 31.93 g/L; however, final OD₆₀₀ decreased by 12.82%, from 22.37 to 19.50 (Fig. 2D). Thus, the further increase in butyrate titer was not accompanied by an increase in final OD₆₀₀. But-6B100 was used as the parent for the final ARTP round.

In the third round, But-6B100 underwent a second ARTP treatment (Fig. 2A). Primary tube screening identified 24 candidates with higher butyrate titers than the separate But-6B100 control (3.59 g/L), out of 100 screened (24.00%; Fig. 2B,C). The selected candidate But-6C100 produced 34.93 g/L butyrate in independent shake-flask validation, a 9.39% increase over But-6B100 (31.93 g/L). Unlike the second round, final OD₆₀₀ increased, rising by 9.74% from 19.50 to 21.40 (Fig. 2D). Across the three rounds, But-6C100 achieved a 58.31% higher shake-flask butyrate titer than the pre-mutagenesis parent But-6 (34.93 versus 22.07 g/L), equivalent to approximately 3.3 times the titer of the original strain But-0 (10.53 g/L; Fig. 2D). The selected strains therefore showed successive increases in butyrate titer despite differences in the direction of the final OD₆₀₀ response. But-6C100 was subsequently used for fermentation-medium optimization.
