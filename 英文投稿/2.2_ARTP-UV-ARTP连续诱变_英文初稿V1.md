### 2.2 Enhanced Butyrate Production through Sequential ARTP–UV–ARTP Mutagenesis

To further improve butyrate production, engineered strain But-6 was subjected to atmospheric and room-temperature plasma (ARTP) mutagenesis in the first round (Fig. 2A). A total of 100 candidate mutants were evaluated by primary tube screening alongside a separate But-6 parental control, which yielded 2.39 g/L butyrate under the screening conditions. Sixteen candidates exceeded the parental control, accounting for 16.00% of the 100 screened mutants (Fig. 2B,C). The candidate with the highest butyrate titer in this screen, designated But-6A100, was selected for independent shake-flask validation. Under shake-flask conditions, But-6A100 produced 27.73 g/L butyrate, a 25.68% increase over But-6 (22.07 g/L). Its final OD₆₀₀ also increased by 70.74%, from 13.10 to 22.37 (Fig. 2D). On the basis of these results, But-6A100 was used as the parent for the subsequent UV mutagenesis round.

In the second round, But-6A100 underwent ultraviolet (UV) mutagenesis (Fig. 2A). Primary tube screening included 100 candidate mutants and a separate But-6A100 parental control with a butyrate titer of 3.09 g/L. Twenty-six candidates outperformed the parent, representing 26.00% of those screened (Fig. 2B,C). But-6B100, the highest-producing candidate in the tube screen, was then examined in independent shake-flask cultures. Its butyrate titer reached 31.93 g/L, 15.14% higher than that of But-6A100 (27.73 g/L), whereas final OD₆₀₀ decreased by 12.82%, from 22.37 to 19.50 (Fig. 2D). Thus, the increase in shake-flask butyrate titer was accompanied by a lower final OD₆₀₀. But-6B100 was selected as the parent for the third mutagenesis round.

The third round returned to ARTP mutagenesis, using But-6B100 as the parental strain (Fig. 2A). Among 100 candidates screened in tubes, 24 produced more butyrate than the separate But-6B100 control (3.59 g/L), corresponding to 24.00% of the candidates (Fig. 2B,C). The top candidate, But-6C100, produced 34.93 g/L butyrate upon independent shake-flask validation, a 9.39% increase over its parent But-6B100 (31.93 g/L). Final OD₆₀₀ increased by 9.74%, from 19.50 to 21.40 (Fig. 2D). Relative to But-6 before mutagenesis, But-6C100 achieved a 58.31% higher shake-flask butyrate titer (34.93 versus 22.07 g/L), equivalent to approximately 3.3 times the titer of the original strain But-0 (10.53 g/L; Fig. 2D). Across the three successive ARTP–UV–ARTP mutagenesis rounds, the selected strains showed stepwise increases in shake-flask butyrate titer, although changes in final OD₆₀₀ did not follow the same pattern. But-6C100 was therefore taken forward for sequential single-factor optimization of the fermentation medium.
