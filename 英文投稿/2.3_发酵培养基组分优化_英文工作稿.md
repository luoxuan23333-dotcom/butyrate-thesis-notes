## I. English manuscript draft (four paragraphs)

### 2.3 Optimization of Fermentation Medium Components for Butyrate Production

To further improve butyrate production by But-6C100, the concentrations of four fermentation medium components were examined sequentially in shake-flask experiments, with the selected level of each component carried forward to the next step. Yeast extract was evaluated first (Fig. 3A). As its concentration increased from 2.5 to 5.0 and 7.5 g/L, the butyrate titer increased from 26.30 to 34.33 and 36.17 g/L, respectively. At 7.5 g/L yeast extract, the titer was 37.52% and 5.34% higher than those obtained at 2.5 and 5.0 g/L, respectively. Further increasing yeast extract to 10.0 g/L reduced the titer to 34.20 g/L. Thus, 7.5 g/L was selected for the subsequent optimization steps as the best-performing yeast extract concentration among the levels tested.

With the selected yeast extract concentration in place, the effect of peptone supplementation was evaluated (Fig. 3B). Increasing peptone from 2.5 to 5.0 g/L raised the butyrate titer from 33.57 to 37.67 g/L, a 12.21% increase. Higher peptone levels did not improve production: titers decreased to 36.77 and 35.27 g/L at 7.5 and 10.0 g/L, respectively. Accordingly, 5.0 g/L peptone was selected for the next step.

Corn steep liquor was then evaluated at five concentrations ranging from 4.0 to 12.0 g/L (Fig. 3C). Butyrate titer increased from 24.30 g/L at 4.0 g/L corn steep liquor to 38.57 g/L at 10.0 g/L, an increase of 58.71%. The titer at 10.0 g/L was also 5.37% higher than that at 8.0 g/L (36.60 g/L). However, a further increase to 12.0 g/L reduced the titer to 36.27 g/L. Among the tested levels, 10.0 g/L corn steep liquor gave the highest butyrate titer and was therefore retained for the final optimization step.

Finally, molasses supplementation was examined (Fig. 3D). Increasing its concentration from 2.5 to 5.0 and 7.5 g/L raised the butyrate titer from 36.53 to 38.30 and 40.47 g/L, respectively. At 7.5 g/L molasses, the titer was 10.77% and 5.66% higher than those obtained at 2.5 and 5.0 g/L, respectively, but decreased to 38.23 g/L when molasses was increased to 10.0 g/L. Based on these sequential single-factor experiments, the selected concentrations of yeast extract, peptone, corn steep liquor, and molasses were 7.5, 5.0, 10.0, and 7.5 g/L, respectively. Under the resulting medium formulation, But-6C100 produced 40.47 g/L butyrate in shake flasks, a 15.84% increase over the titer obtained with the basal medium before optimization (34.93 g/L; Fig. 3D). This formulation was subsequently used for the 5 L bioreactor experiments.

**三条重点事实边界**：

1. **是逐次递进的单因素试验（sequential one-factor-at-a-time experiments）**：方法1.5写明上一组分选定的最佳浓度会作为下一组分的基础条件。没有多因素全交叉组合试验、Plackett–Burman筛选、RSM/响应面或交互作用验证。英文只能说`selected concentrations / best among the tested levels`，不能宣称数学意义上的global optimum、联合交互作用或`synergy`。
2. **不同图3面板测量处于不同优化阶段**：不能将某面板的最低值与另一面板的最高值直接当作唯一变量变化的效果。例如酵母粉优化36.17和蛋白胨5.0时37.67不能自称为“蛋白胨直接增产4.1%”，因为两次所在培养基背景可能不同。
3. **指标类型**：全部图3纵轴是butyrate g/L浓度，写`titer`，不是`yield (g/g)`或`productivity (g/(L·h))`。未找到本稿原始显著性检验输出，英文没新增`significantly`。所有报告百分比保留Word原稿，未从展示均值推算替换。

## III. 英文文献逐段学习：哪些规则可迁移，哪些不能

### [Kou et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40643-024-00742-y) — 丁酸培养基单因素原始研究（出版商HTML相关章节）

- **实际核到的写法**：在Methods先明确基本培养基、要变化的组分及测定指标；Results以`Single factor optimization of fermentation medium components`为标题，将每个组分的增补量与对应产酸值和图分区配对，然后再交代后续PB筛选和RSM。
- **本稿适用**：保留四个步骤的先后顺序；先报告观察到的浓度梯度，再写被选中的水平与下一步衔接。不要每个自然段都使用冗余、未经统计验证的因果结论。
- **必须避免误借**：Kou24研究*Clostridium beijerinckii*秸秆半固态发酵，研究还进行了PB、最陡爬坡、RSM和正交试验；**本稿只有顺序单因素**，不能照搬“statistically significant factor”“response-surface optimum”或其浓度数值。

### [2023年*Butyribacterium methylotrophicum*原始研究](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13068-023-02263-w) — 氮源对发酵产物的影响（出版商HTML相关段）

- **段落功能**：先明确为何研究氮源→说明比较的具体氮源和等氮条件→用Fig1给出的终点产品及生长测定支持比较→最后限定在该宿主/培养底物下的结论。其后续机制段落另有氨基酸与转录组实测；不能提前混入纯结果描述。
- **本稿适用**：`corn steep liquor`为正确的原文领域术语；最好写`corn steep liquor supplementation`的加量梯度。现稿是添加量不是等氮替换，不能写`replaced yeast extract with CSL`。
- **必须避免误借**：对方采用甲醇同化途径与等氮替换条件、并有氨基酸及转录组分析；不能因为“CSL富含赖氨酸”就声称本稿But-6C100由特定氨基酸或转运蛋白机制增产。

### [Huang et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11814-011-0110-9) — 糖蜜主要糖组分研究（期刊摘要级）

- **已核摘要**：以*Clostridium tyrobutyricum*为研究对象，讨论糖蜜中葡萄糖/果糖/蔗糖对丁酸形成的不同作用，同时选择玉米浆作氮源；摘要中的外部实测值与本稿无关。
