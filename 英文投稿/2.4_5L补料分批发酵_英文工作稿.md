## I. Section 2.4 — Complete English candidate (three paragraphs)

### 2.4 Butyrate Production by Fed-Batch Fermentation in a 5 L Bioreactor

To evaluate butyrate production by mutant strain But-6C100 under controlled bioreactor conditions, fed-batch fermentation was performed in a 5 L stirred-tank bioreactor using the four medium component concentrations selected in Section 2.3 (Fig. 4A). The fermentation comprised an initial batch phase (0–24 h), followed by a glucose-feeding phase (24–48 h; Fig. 4B). During the batch phase, residual glucose concentration decreased from 51.13 to 1.83 g/L, while OD₆₀₀ increased from 1.13 to 45.43. Meanwhile, the butyrate titer reached 33.43 g/L at 24 h (Fig. 4B), corresponding to an average volumetric productivity of 1.39 g/(L·h) during this phase (Fig. 4C). Continuous glucose feeding was initiated at 24 h, after which residual glucose was maintained between 1.83 and 3.93 g/L, within the targeted range of 1–5 g/L.

During the feeding phase (24–48 h), OD₆₀₀ continued to increase and reached a maximum of 52.70 at 36 h, when the butyrate titer was 46.63 g/L (Fig. 4B). Subsequently, OD₆₀₀ declined to 41.40 at 48 h, whereas butyrate continued to accumulate, reaching 58.63 g/L. The net increase in butyrate concentration over the final 12 h was 12.00 g/L. The average volumetric productivity calculated for the feeding phase was 1.05 g/(L·h) (Fig. 4C). Thus, butyrate accumulation continued after the maximum OD₆₀₀ had been reached, although the measured optical density declined during the later stage of fermentation.

After 48 h, the final butyrate titer reached 58.63 g/L, with an overall average volumetric productivity of 1.22 g/(L·h) (Fig. 4B,C). The reported butyrate yield was 0.59 g/g. Compared with the titers obtained in shake-flask batch cultures containing the basal medium (34.93 g/L) and the optimized medium (40.47 g/L), the final bioreactor titer was higher by 67.84% and 44.89%, respectively (Fig. 4D). It was also 5.6-fold the titer of the original starting strain But-0 (10.53 g/L; Fig. 5). These findings demonstrate sustained butyrate accumulation in the 5 L fed-batch experiment, including the period after OD₆₀₀ began to decrease, and support further evaluation of the process at larger scales.

### B. 0.59 g/g（Q-01）：当前无法验证，且涉及科学解释

1. 中文2.4仅写`得率为0.59 g/g`，1.6只给出**初始葡萄糖50.0 g/L、0–24h分批培养、24–48h高浓度葡萄糖连续流加、剩余葡萄糖控制1–5 g/L**；没有报告**补料液总重量/体积、补料葡萄糖浓度、最终体积、加碱总量、取样体积、实际总糖消耗量及糖蜜参与的可发酵碳源质量**。
2. 用C₄₈=58.63 g/L与0.59 g/g简单倒算仅得到“约99.4 g/L的**假设分母等效值**”，**不是已测的葡萄糖消耗量**。在加料、加碱、取样都会改变体系体积的fed-batch过程中，产物质量应按`C₄₈×V₄₈`并考虑初始残留、取样排出等；总消耗糖须按初始投入+补料实际质量−末次及取样残糖计算；其它碳源须定义是否包括在分母。
3. [Wang et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13068-019-1408-9)把yield明确写成`g butyrate per g glucose consumed`；其论文给出的**葡萄糖单一底物、特定代谢产物平衡的理论得率约0.49 g/g**。本稿0.59 g/g若被声称同口径，仅凭数值大小就值得要求检查分母、补料/糖蜜和分析质量衡算；**不能因此直接判定错误**，因为本稿有糖蜜、酵母提取物、玉米浆等复杂碳源且供氧与宿主不同。
4. [Qureshi et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/fermentation8100491)在Methods明确将产物浓度、平均体积生产强度`g/(L·h)`和以**消耗葡萄糖为分母**的`g/g`得率分开；不能仅见单位g/g就自动认定消耗葡萄糖定义与本稿一致。
5. **建议作者/导师核实来源后决定的最终英语**：若按完整可发酵底物消耗，则写`butyrate yield of 0.59 g per g total fermentable substrate consumed`（必须有各类底物计量记录）；若按葡萄糖消耗，则写`0.59 g butyrate per g glucose consumed`（必须有总加料和残糖物料衡算）；若没有记录，最好从投稿正文中**暂不把该得率作为已证明的核心指标**，图5/摘要/结论同样需要一致性处理。

- 摇瓶条件1.2.4/1.5：**30 °C、72 h、基础葡萄糖30.0 g/L**；反应器1.6：**37 °C、48 h、起始葡萄糖50.0 g/L、pH7.0、1.0 vvm、24h以后流加**。因此+67.84%/+44.89%是**不同培养系统/条件下的终点滴度对比**，**不能以此证明纯“反应器体积放大”或单独fed-batch策略导致了全部提升**。

- 保留“后期丁酸仍持续净积累”的确定观察，但不写`industrial scalability was demonstrated`：一个实验室5 L额定罐、2.5 L初始工作体积不足以证明工业生产可行性、经济性或长期稳定性。
