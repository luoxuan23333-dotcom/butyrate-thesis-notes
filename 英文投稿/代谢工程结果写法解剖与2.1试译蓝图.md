### 2.1 Construction of the Butyrate Biosynthetic Pathway

*Bacillus megaterium* But-0 was used as the starting strain for stepwise metabolic engineering. Genes associated with competing by-product pathways were progressively deleted, and heterologous butyrate biosynthetic genes were integrated at the corresponding chromosomal loci by CRISPR-Cas9-mediated homologous recombination. This strategy was designed to reduce by-product formation and increase butyrate production (Fig. 1A).

To reduce lactate formation while introducing an additional step in the butyrate biosynthetic pathway, the lactate dehydrogenase gene *ldhA* was deleted from But-0, and the *atoB* gene encoding acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase from *Escherichia coli* was integrated at the same locus, yielding strain But-1. The butyrate titer reached 11.63 g/L, compared with 10.53 g/L in But-0 (reported increase, 10.44%), while lactate concentration decreased from 4.30 to 1.03 g/L (reported decrease, 75.97%; Fig. 1B). The OD₆₀₀ decreased from 14.97 to 12.37, whereas the mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids increased from 41.7% to 47.7% (Fig. 1B,C). Nevertheless, acetate and succinate remained at 5.23 and 6.47 g/L, respectively, indicating substantial residual by-product accumulation.

To further reduce acetate accumulation, the acetate kinase gene *ackA* was deleted from But-1, and the *hbd* gene encoding 3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase from *Clostridium acetobutylicum* was integrated at the same locus to construct But-2. Its butyrate titer increased to 12.67 g/L (reported increase over But-1, 8.88%), while acetate concentration declined from 5.23 to 3.50 g/L (reported decrease, 33.12%). Correspondingly, the butyrate-to-acetate mass concentration ratio increased from 2.22 to 3.62 (Fig. 1B). Lactate and succinate concentrations decreased to 0.60 and 4.73 g/L, respectively, and the mass fraction of butyrate among the measured organic acids increased to 58.9% (Fig. 1B,C). In parallel, OD₆₀₀ increased from 12.37 to 13.77 (Fig. 1B). Thus, this combined modification was associated with greater butyrate accumulation, lower acetate accumulation, and an increased proportion of butyrate among the measured organic acids.
